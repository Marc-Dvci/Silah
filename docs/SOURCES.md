## Stated inputs, shown with every number that uses them

- **Assumption: carrier frequency of 1 in 1,000 for panel genes without a published Emirati figure. It is the highest average that keeps the panel's share of the added chance for untested first cousins within the published 1.7 to 2.8 in 100 (Hamamy 2012), given the seven published Emirati figures.** A stated assumption, shown with every number that uses it. 1 in 200 would imply 9.8 added children in 100 for untested first cousins from panel genes alone (docs/SENSITIVITY.md).
- **When a result does not state the lab's detection, Silah gives remaining chances as a range, from 99% down to 80% of a gene's disease-causing changes detected. DoH does not publish per-gene detection rates.** A stated range, not a personal figure: lower detection gives a higher remaining chance.
- **Detection stated in the lab-signed result: the share of each gene's disease-causing changes the lab's panel detects, and, gene by gene, whether the partner's sample was checked for the person's exact change. In this demonstration the lab is simulated and its rates are example values: 90% for the panel, 99% for a check of an exact change.** A deployment takes these from the lab's validation report, through the result format (schema/result-bundle.schema.json).
- **Detection as printed on the report the person brought (for example in its Methods section) and confirmed by them on the phone. It is not checked against the laboratory's validation until the counsellor sees the original report.** Without a stated figure Silah gives ranges (99% to 80% detection).
