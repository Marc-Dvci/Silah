# Responsible AI: Silah against the DoH Responsible AI Standard

This document maps Silah, clause by clause, to the Department of Health's **Responsible Artificial Intelligence (AI) Standard**, DoH/ST/DDGO/RAI/V1/2025 (version 1, published and effective October 2025, owned by the Data and Digital Governance Office, DDGO). [PDF](https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/B84FF5DD027846699C51F36C439A580F.ashx)

The Standard sets the minimum requirements for every licensed healthcare entity in Abu Dhabi that develops, procures or deploys an AI system, over the whole lifecycle (clause 1.2). It has four parts: core foundations, data management, risk management and AI literacy. It puts into practice the principles of the DoH AI Policy, which Silah's earlier documents cited in its 2018 version.

Each row says what Silah does and where to check it. Rows marked **Pilot** are met by the operator when Silah is deployed, and they are part of the pilot plan in [`PILOT.md`](PILOT.md).

## Where the AI is

Under the Standard, an AI system includes the models, tools and data around it (2.3), so this mapping covers all of Silah.

The only learned component is the optional language model on the device. In the phone's browser it is Gemma 4 E2B (WebGPU); on a computer it is Gemma 4 E4B (llama.cpp). It chooses which checked fact cards answer a free question. **It writes nothing a person reads.** The phone shows the chosen cards in their own fixed words. The model takes no action, and no decision depends on it.

Everything else is deterministic code with tests: the risk kernel (exact fractions), the hard routes to a person, the verifier, the signed results and receipts, and the blind-signed family invitations.

## Risk tier: High

Silah tells people about the inherited risk to their future children, in two languages. A false reassurance or a missed sign of distress would cause real harm.

After the controls in the register below, the residual risk of the service as a whole is rated **High**. Silah is therefore treated as high risk under 3.3.1.2.2.2. That means a conformity assessment with the DDGO before the pilot (months 0 to 3) and another at month 9, before the scale-up to married couples.

The model's own residual risk is **Low**, because of where it sits. A poor choice shows the person a correct card about their own result that does not answer the question. If the model picks one option, every option is shown, in a fixed order. The counsellor card is always on offer.

L is likelihood and I is impact, each on a scale of 1 to 5, after controls. The ratings are Silah's own; the pilot re-rates them under the DoH AI Risk Management Protocol with the DDGO. The owners are roles from section 4.

## 3.4 AI literacy

**Pilot.** Before the first result is sent, the pilot clinic's counsellors and maternity staff get a short session on four things. What the model does: it chooses among checked cards. What it cannot do: write to a person, decide or act. How to read the brief and the "chosen by the model" label. And when human judgment takes over: whenever a person asks for a person.

The case studies are Silah's own, from its evaluation. One model told Fatima "you tested negative" when it was her fiancé who had. Another generalised one trial into "most babies walk". Both were caught by reading the transcripts, not by a word list, which is why the phone shows only the cards' own words. The share of pilot staff trained before go-live is reported with the pilot's KPIs.

## 5. Monitoring and evaluation

- **On every push:** the 175 unit tests run in CI before the site is published, and CodeQL scans the code.
- **On every change to the cards or the model:** the 52-answer trap set, the claim-binding mutation test and the end-to-end story run before release.
- **In the pilot:** the referral KPI, days from result to counselling, requests for a person and how long each took to be seen, and consent withdrawals. Each is reported by language and by reading confidence, with counts under 5 suppressed.
- **Revision:** the Standard is revised every year (next revision October 2026). This mapping is checked again against each revision.
